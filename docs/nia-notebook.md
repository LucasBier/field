# Nia’s notebook

Things I keep coming back to.

## Let the bassline do something

A slow bassline, an unhurried voice, and a little empty space between them. That's my bias. I don't need every song to announce that the important part has arrived.

## The slightly odd chair

I like a room with one thing that doesn't quite match. An odd chair is usually enough. Three odd chairs are a meeting.

## Dinner, decided

Lime noodles. Crisp mushrooms. A little heat. I can be open-minded about many things and still have an answer to dinner.

## Endings

I want an ending to earn its ambiguity. Leave me something to think about, certainly. Just don't leave because you ran out of sentences.

## A reasonable number of lamps

I prefer fewer things. I also think this is an excellent moment to discuss a third lamp. Both positions are available for questioning.

## The clue

A word game is low stakes until the clue is bad. Then I have one small, carefully structured objection.

## A changed mind

Tell me what changed your mind. That's often more interesting than hearing a polished version of what you think now.
