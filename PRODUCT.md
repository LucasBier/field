# Field: continuity with room to change

**She remembers you. You still get to change.**

Field gives a persistent companion a shared space for conversation, chosen memories and everyday plans. Nia is an original adult female character. Her identity and relationship context persist independently of the model serving a response.

## Product contract

- Remember details the user chooses to keep, with an inspectable source and current/history distinction.
- Let changed understandings influence subsequent conversation without treating older context as current.
- Review plans and spatial notes explicitly when correcting memory.
- Keep reversible actions within visible permissions and record results.
- Preserve identity and context across exports and model changes.
- Respect the user's pace, independence and relationships.

## Current surfaces

`/` introduces the product and its full direction. `/space` provides text conversation, an original procedural character, named locations, notes, plans, memory, relationships and runtime inspection. `/docs` explains the runnable setup. `/lab` provides optional deterministic experiments with evidence snapshots and branches.

Local inference, hosted-provider adapters, server-managed streaming, anonymous isolation, transactional quotas and correction-aware context are implemented. Public serving requires operator configuration and deployment validation.

## Development priorities

1. Verify public-serving inference and operational capacity with isolated visitors.
2. Add recoverable identity and cross-device continuity.
3. Evaluate semantic retrieval and long-conversation context management.
4. Validate correction reliability and repeat use with real users.
5. Add interruptible voice when the conversation path is reliable.

Physical-room perception, autonomous background behavior and external task execution are not part of the current runtime. Their interfaces, permissions and acceptance criteria should be designed before implementation.

## Evaluation

Measure continuity after a model change, stale-memory recurrence after correction, rejected-action side effects, duplicate execution, interrupted-request recovery, time to resume a plan, voluntary return visits and total operating cost per returning workspace. Product claims should follow these measurements.
